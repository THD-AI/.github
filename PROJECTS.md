# THD AI – project directory

Explore the visual [project showroom](https://thd-ai.github.io/) or [share your own work](SHOWCASE.md). The first projects below get the collection started; they are not the limits of what belongs here.

Choose a project that interests you, read its setup and contribution instructions, and start with a small change. Community forks preserve the original authors and licences. They offer a place to collaborate through THD AI; the upstream repositories remain the original projects.

## Start something

- [Student project starter](https://github.com/THD-AI/AIN-B-0-Template-Repository-SS-23) – use the template and adapt it to your project. Its historical URL is retained.
- [GitHub beginner's guide](https://github.com/THD-AI/GitHub-Guide-for-Beginners) – learn branches, pull requests, and collaboration.
- [Community hub](https://github.com/THD-AI/AIN-Community-Hub-WS23) – the repository behind organization Discussions. Open to every cohort despite its historical URL.

Members can create repositories and lead their own projects. Introduce yours in [Ideas](https://github.com/orgs/THD-AI/discussions/categories/ideas); share a working result in [Show and tell](https://github.com/orgs/THD-AI/discussions/categories/show-and-tell). See [how to join and contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Earlier projects and courses

- [Devablos V2](https://github.com/THD-AI/AIN-B-10-Internet-Technologies) – archived React and Node.js chatbot project. The original code and branding are preserved; fork it if you want to continue it.
- [Devablos prototype](https://github.com/THD-AI/AIN-B-10-Internet-Technologies-Prototype) – earlier chatbot iteration, retained as project history.
- The first-semester course repositories remain dated archives where they contain actual student work. They are not current course documentation. Empty second-semester template copies were removed after local backup.
- Historical lecture-transcriber forks are retained as archives, with their original upstream provenance.

Private projects are not listed here. Their visibility is unchanged. This directory is a starting point, not a claim that every experiment is actively maintained.
