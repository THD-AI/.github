# THD AI – visual direction and credits

## Direction

An open campus, soft light, and room for people to build together. The visual family follows THD Room Finder: lavender haze, dusty blue, peach sunlight, muted sage, and students represented as flat cool-grey silhouettes. It is illustrated campus realism, not pixel art.

Use the open-portal symbol at small sizes. The first typographic avatar is preserved in the versioned archive. Use the campus banner on the organization profile and community home. Keep personal projects' existing one-line drawing artwork and their authorship; community forks are not rebrands of the original author's profile.

| Token | Colour | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#242667` | Text and avatar background |
| Lavender | `#E8E6FA` | Quiet surfaces |
| Blue | `#A9B7EC` | Supporting detail |
| Peach | `#D8BDBB` | Warm accent |
| Silhouette | `#9AA0A8` | People, without identifiable detail |

The words **THD AI** and **Build something together.** identify this independent project-sharing space. Do not use an official university seal or imply endorsement.

## Campus banner source and licence

`assets/thd-ai-banner.png` is adapted from the campus illustration in [THD Room Finder](https://github.com/arudaev/THD-Room-Finder/blob/main/design/roadmap/README.md). That illustration is a repaint of [Hauptcampus der Technischen Hochschule Deggendorf 2018](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hauptcampus_der_Technischen_Hochschule_Deggendorf_2018.jpg), by **Danube-Waidler**, licensed **[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)**. This banner adaptation is shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. Changes: wider composition, lighter lavender and blue atmosphere, simplified grey silhouettes, and THD AI lettering. Attribution must accompany reuse.

The banner was made with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool using the campus illustration as the architecture reference and Room Finder's `hero-day.webp` and `batch.webp` as style references. The finished asset is committed here. No identifiable student faces are used.

### Reproduction brief

Repaint the real-campus-derived architectural composition in the THD Room Finder illustration family. Preserve the buildings, paths, windows, trees, seats, and proportions; do not invent architecture. Use pale lavender haze, dusty cornflower blue, warm peach afternoon light, muted sage vegetation, and subtle painterly texture. Render people as flat light cool-grey silhouettes around `#9AA0A8`, without faces, clothing details, or internal shading. Compose a 3:1 panorama with buildings across the lower half and sky above. Set the exact words “THD AI” and “Build something together.” in deep indigo sans-serif in the upper left. No university seal, robots, AI brains, neon, or pixel art.

## Open-portal symbol

`assets/thd-ai-symbol-v2.png` is the current transparent symbol: an asymmetric open architectural portal with a warm sun at its centre. It suggests room for a person, an idea, and what comes next. Deep indigo, periwinkle, and peach connect it to the campus illustrations. It replaces the boxed initials and is shared under CC BY 4.0. It is independent artwork, without university marks.
