# Community conduct

THD AI is a place to learn and build with other students. Beginners, different cohorts, disciplines, and backgrounds are welcome.

- Discuss ideas respectfully. Give specific, constructive feedback and credit the people whose work you use.
- No harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, spam, or promotion unrelated to the discussion.
- Do not publish someone else's personal information, private messages, credentials, or university access keys.
- Respect licences, academic integrity, and a person's decision not to participate.

Project maintainers moderate their spaces. Organization owners can remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or remove access when needed. They should explain moderation decisions privately when practical and protect the person reporting a problem.

Report a concern privately to [Alex Rudaev](mailto:alex.rudaev@outlook.com). Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. If the concern involves Alex, contact another organization owner through GitHub.
