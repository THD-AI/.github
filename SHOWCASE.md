# Put your work on the shelf

THD AI is an independent showroom for personal and university projects by people connected to THD. It exists to make thoughtful work easier to find and give people a way to collaborate in their own time.

## Share something you care about

Use the [project submission form](https://github.com/THD-AI/thd-ai.github.io/issues/new?template=project-submission.yml). Your repository can remain in your own account; transferring or forking it into THD AI is optional.

Tell people what the project does, why you made it, who worked on it, and how they can understand or try it. A screenshot, working demo, or clear setup instructions helps. State whether it is an experiment, course project, working tool, or historical work, and explain limitations. Say whether you want feedback, contributors, a new maintainer, or simply to share something you enjoyed making.

A small useful experiment is enough. AI assistance is welcome, and so is work made without it. Quality, care, creator credit, and honest descriptions matter more than the choice of tools.

## How it gets published

The submission workflow acknowledges the issue and checks whether the linked repository can be read publicly. It does not approve or publish a project automatically. A maintainer reviews the listing and image permission, then adds it through a pull request to the website catalogue. The site checks must pass before publication. Creators can request corrections or removal at any time.

Share only work you created or have permission to represent. Keep credentials, private coursework, personal documents, and restricted teaching material out of public submissions. Preserve existing licences and credit every collaborator and source.

Not ready to submit? Bring an idea or work in progress to [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/THD-AI/discussions/categories/ideas). For membership or a private question, [email Alex](mailto:alex.rudaev@outlook.com).
